### Recording Flow (6 clicks total)
The "Send" button steps through the demo. Step counter (bottom-right) reads Step 1/6 → 6/6.

1. **Click "Send" (1/6):** Sarah describes her sustainable fashion business → Compass analyzes + starts searching
2. **Auto-progression:** Compass announces 4 matches → mentor cards slide into the right pane (staggered)
3. **Click "Send" (2/6):** Sarah asks how to connect with Margaret → **Mentor Profile card appears** — *click the ▶ to play the intro video, let it finish, pause 1–2s*
4. **Click "Send" (3/6):** Sarah approves → **AI-drafted introduction** appears in the right pane
5. **Click "Send" (4/6):** Sarah sends it → "Margaret accepted!" → **scheduling grid** appears (Tue 2 PM highlighted)
6. **Click "Send" (5/6):** Sarah picks the time → **Session Confirmed** + prep checklist
7. **Click "Send" (6/6):** Sarah asks what's next → **Mentorship Dashboard** (final hold frame)

**Total interaction time:** ~2–2.5 minutes (with the video clip playback)

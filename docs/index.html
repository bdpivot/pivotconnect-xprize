Judge-facing walkthrough

A clear, evidence-aware demo of how AI could coordinate mentorship access without pretending the demo is live AI.

The flow below is the same every time: a founder provides a synthetic intake, the demo shows explainable mentor candidates with caveats, a human approves the represented introduction, and the follow-up view shows a prepared meeting path. All of that is label-safe and reviewable offline.

Truth note: this is an interactive product prototype. It is designed to help judges inspect the workflow, not to claim production users, live mentors, or live Gemini inference.

Step 4 • preparation and follow-up

Meeting prep, follow-up, and evidence summary

Once the intro is approved, the demo shows the next step: a prepared first meeting and a simple follow-up cadence. The content stays synthetic and readable.

Preparation checklist

  • Clarify the founder’s goal for the first 30 minutes.
  • Confirm which topics are in scope for the meeting.
  • Prepare 3 guiding questions and 2 tradeoff prompts.
  • Capture one next action and one follow-up date.

Follow-up cadence

  • Send a short recap after the meeting.
  • Track one milestone per week for 12 weeks.
  • Keep the founder in control of any personal sharing.
  • Record overrides and reasons for later review.

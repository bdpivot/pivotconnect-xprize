# LaunchPad AI Demo Script for Xprize
**For HeyGen Video Production**

---

## Demo Narrative Arc
**Duration:** 3-5 minutes  
**Presenter:** Ciara (HeyGen avatar)  
**Tone:** Confident, accessible, inspiring

---

## Scene 1: The Problem (30 seconds)

**Ciara speaks:**
> "Millions of aspiring entrepreneurs have great ideas but lack access to the coaching, mentorship, and financial expertise needed to succeed. Traditional incubators serve maybe 5% of applicants. What about everyone else?"

**Screen shows:**
- Statistics: 27M small businesses in US, only ~200K get incubator support
- Map showing rural/underserved areas with few resources

---

## Scene 2: The Solution (45 seconds)

**Ciara speaks:**
> "Meet LaunchPad AI — a multi-agent coaching system that makes entrepreneurship support accessible to anyone, anywhere. Powered by Google Gemini, it combines the wisdom of experienced entrepreneurs with AI that never sleeps, never judges, and scales infinitely."

**Screen shows:**
- LaunchPad AI logo + tagline
- Agent roster: Phoenix, Atlas, Ledger, Compass, Catalyst
- Simple architecture diagram

---

## Scene 3: Live Demo - Coaching Conversation (90 seconds)

**Ciara speaks:**
> "Let me show you Phoenix in action. Meet Sarah, a first-time entrepreneur with a sustainable fashion idea."

**Screen shows chat interface:**

**User:** "I want to start a sustainable fashion business, but I don't know where to begin."

**Phoenix 🦅:** "Welcome to LaunchPad AI! It's great to have you here. Starting a sustainable fashion business is not just commercial — it's a statement about the world you want to create. Let's break this down into clear next steps..."

[Phoenix provides structured advice: market research, business model, initial validation steps]

**User:** "What's the market size for sustainable fashion?"

**Phoenix 🦅:** "Great question! Let me connect you with Atlas, our market research specialist."

[Shows routing to Atlas agent]

**Atlas 🗺️:** [Delivers market sizing data, growth trends, key segments]

**User:** "How much money do I need to get started?"

**Phoenix 🦅:** "Let me bring in Ledger to help model this out."

**Ledger 📊:** [Provides startup cost breakdown, runway calculation, funding scenarios]

---

## Scene 4: The Technology (45 seconds)

**Ciara speaks:**
> "Under the hood, LaunchPad AI uses Google's Vertex AI platform with Gemini 2.5 Pro for complex reasoning and Gemini 2.0 Flash for instant responses. The Phoenix orchestrator intelligently routes questions to specialized agents — just like a real Chief of Staff would delegate to domain experts."

**Screen shows:**
- Technology stack diagram
- Function calling visualization
- Agent handoff flow
- Code execution for financial modeling

**Ciara speaks:**

LaunchPad AI serves entrepreneurs who would never make it into a traditional incubator — veterans transitioning to civilian life, single parents building side hustles, artists turning passion into business, farmers adopting precision agriculture."

**Screen shows:**
- User testimonial quotes (mockups for now)
- Diversity of supported programs
- Geographic reach map

Whether you're an aspiring entrepreneur, an experienced mentor, or an investor who believes in universal opportunity — we want to work with you. Visit pivotintl.org to learn more, and follow our journey as we compete for the Xprize AI Tutor Prize."

## Technical Requirements for Screen Recording

### Demo Environment Setup
1. **Clean browser window** - pivotintl.org homepage ready
2. **Chat interface mockup** - Can use existing OpenClaw chat UI or build simple web interface
3. **Terminal window** - Show `npm run dev` output briefly
4. **Architecture slides** - Create in Canva/Figma

### Conversation Flow to Record
```
User: "I want to start a sustainable fashion business"
→ Phoenix: Coaching response (15 seconds)

User: "What's the market size?"
→ Phoenix: "Let me connect you with Atlas"
→ Atlas: Market research (10 seconds)

User: "How much money do I need?"
→ Phoenix: "Bringing in Ledger"
→ Ledger: Financial breakdown (10 seconds)
```

### Supporting Visuals Needed
1. **PHI Logo** - High resolution
2. **Agent Icons** - Phoenix 🦅, Atlas 🗺️, Ledger 📊, etc.
3. **Architecture Diagram** - User → Phoenix → Specialized Agents
4. **Statistics Graphics** - Market opportunity data
5. **Program Portfolio Grid** - 40+ programs visual
6. **Geographic Map** - Showing reach/impact
